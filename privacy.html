2. Data We Process

This website does not collect personal data through any contact form. When you visit our site, the following technical data is processed automatically:

  • IP address (partially anonymised by Google Analytics)
  • Device and browser information (User-Agent)
  • Pages visited, session duration, and traffic source
  • Server access logs generated by GitHub Pages hosting infrastructure

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Analysing visitor statistics and improving site performance
  • Operating the technical infrastructure securely

4. Legal Basis

Analytical cookies are only activated after you provide explicit consent (KVKK Article 5/1; explicit consent). Technical infrastructure logs are processed on the basis of our legitimate interests in operating the site securely (KVKK Article 5/2-f).

5. Data Transfers

Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, USA): Analytics data is transmitted to Google's servers. Through Consent Mode, no analytics data is transferred unless you have given consent.

GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA): This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you access our pages, your IP address and browser information are technically transmitted to GitHub servers. This is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

Shopier (Değer Teknoloji A.Ş., Turkey): Product sales are processed through the Shopier platform. Shopier acts as an independent data controller and processes payment and order data under its own privacy policy. Once you are redirected to Shopier from our site, Shopier's data practices apply.

Your data is not shared with any other third parties beyond the service providers described above.

6. Retention Period

Analytics data is retained according to Google's retention settings (up to 14 months by default). GitHub Pages server log retention is governed by GitHub's data policy. If you withdraw your consent, analytical cookies are deactivated immediately.

7. Cookie Policy

This website uses only Google Analytics 4 analytical cookies to analyse visitor statistics. These cookies are only activated upon your consent; if you decline, no analytics data is collected.

You may also disable Google Analytics cookies using the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on. You can update your cookie preferences at any time via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.

8. Your Rights

Under KVKK Article 11, you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information if it has been processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties to whom data is transferred domestically or abroad
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data
  • Request erasure or destruction under the conditions set out in the law
  • Request notification of corrections or erasure to third parties
  • Object to a result arising solely from automated analysis of your data
  • Claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing

9. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise your rights under KVKK Article 11, please contact us in writing at bilgi@ataeymir.com.tr. Please include your full name, contact details, and a clear description of your request. Requests will be responded to within 30 days.
